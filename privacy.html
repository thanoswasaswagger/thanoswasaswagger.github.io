Privacy Policy

Effective: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ASHBIZCON handles information in connection with its software products and public website.

CMS Vault

CMS Vault is local-first. The current edition has no ASHBIZCON backend for Framer CMS content, no remote snapshot storage operated by ASHBIZCON, no CMS-content analytics, and no advertising tracker. Imported JSON or CSV files are parsed in the plugin interface, and exported snapshots/reports are downloaded to the user's device.

Optional recent snapshot history may be stored locally in the plugin/browser environment and can be cleared by the user. Framer platform processing remains subject to Framer's own terms and privacy practices.

Payments

Purchases may be processed by Paddle as merchant of record. Paddle may collect buyer identity, billing, payment, tax, and transaction information as necessary to process a purchase, issue invoices, manage refunds, and comply with legal obligations. ASHBIZCON does not receive or store full payment-card details.

Support

If you contact ASHBIZCON for support, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, product name, screenshots, issue descriptions, or diagnostic details. Do not include passwords, payment-card data, or unnecessary confidential information.

Website

The current public website is a static information site. ASHBIZCON does not intentionally run behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking on this site. Hosting providers may process technical request data such as IP addresses, timestamps, and browser information for security and delivery purposes.

Data retention

Information voluntarily submitted for support may be retained as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain security records, or comply with legal obligations. Local CMS Vault data remains under the user's control unless the user deliberately shares it.

Your choices

You can avoid providing optional support information, clear CMS Vault's local history where available, and contact us with privacy questions through the Contact page.

Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. The effective date above indicates the current version.

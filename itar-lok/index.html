Android shopping app for physical spiritual products

Itar Lok lets customers discover and purchase physical products such as malas, itar, yantras and pooja samagri. Customers sign in before checkout, place an order in the app and complete payment securely through Razorpay.

Product categories

MalaItarYantraPooja SamagriSpiritual Products

Live product catalog

Product names, images, descriptions, prices and availability are managed centrally and shown inside the Android app.

Secure customer accounts

Customers use verified email accounts to access account and order features before completing payments.

Secure online payments

Orders are created server-side and payments are processed using Razorpay. Payment verification is handled by secure backend functions.

App availability

Android app: The current production build is being prepared for direct APK distribution and Google Play publication. This page is the official public web presence for the Itar Lok app and the products sold through it.

Customer support & policies

For questions about orders, products, payments, returns or account access, customers can contact the business through the contact details provided inside the Itar Lok app. Payment, refund, privacy and terms information is presented as part of the app and checkout experience.
